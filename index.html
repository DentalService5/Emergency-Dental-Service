Common Dental Emergencies We Treat

Severe toothache relief is the most common reason patients call us. Pain can come from deep decay, a cracked tooth, a lost filling or an abscess. We also handle knocked-out teeth, chipped teeth, swelling and gum infections. Early care often saves the tooth and prevents a small problem from becoming a costly one.

Some cases need an emergency tooth extraction, while others can be saved with an emergency root canal or a same-day root canal. After a careful exam and X-rays, we recommend the option that protects your health and fits your needs.

What to Do Before You Arrive

Rinse your mouth with warm water and use a cold compress on the cheek to reduce swelling. Avoid placing aspirin directly on the gum. If a tooth was knocked out, hold it by the crown, rinse gently without scrubbing and keep it moist in milk until you reach us. Then call 657-225-0132 so we can prepare for your visit.

If you have trouble breathing or swallowing, or facial swelling spreads quickly, go to the nearest hospital emergency room right away.
